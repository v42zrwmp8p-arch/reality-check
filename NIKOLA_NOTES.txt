NIKOLA / VECTOIQ: HISTORY AND TERMINAL STATUS

The example remains in our ten-record dataset. A failed Yahoo download does not
mean the historical recommendation should disappear from the study.

What is confirmed
- The video date in our supplied notes is 8 May 2020, when VectoIQ common stock
  traded as VTIQ. The symbol changed to NKLA on 4 June 2020 after the merger.
- Nikola filed for Chapter 11 on 19 February 2025.
- FINRA's 24 December 2025 notice confirms a liquidation plan effective on
  12 December 2025, cancelling existing equity interests without a distribution
  for those interests. The confirmed terminal value of the common stock is zero.
- We retain this documented corporate outcome separately from market quotes.
  The exact market-processing date is not inferred from the plan's legal date.

Historical-source search
- Yahoo Finance did not return a history under NKLA, NKLAQ or VTIQ.
- CompaniesMarketCap's public chart exposes only 82 sampled observations. It
  does not give the exact next-trading-day entry after the video. These samples
  have not been substituted for daily prices or interpolated into a daily chart.
- ChartExchange's Nasdaq page returned daily observations starting in September
  2023 in the retrieved page; its OTC page also has 2025 quotes. We have not
  recovered a complete, consistently adjusted series starting in May 2020.

How the results currently handle the case
- The case-status table shows the historical-data limitation and terminal share
  value of USD 0.
- Fixed-horizon returns, drawdown and a continuous chart remain uncomputed.
- No obsolete last quote is carried forward as a current market value.
- Once a complete series is recovered, ticker continuity and the 2024 reverse
